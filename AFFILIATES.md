## Official airports and individual lots — not affiliate offers

Links to official reservation sites (for example parking.jfkairport.com and parking.flylax.com) and to an individual operator’s own booking page are ordinary public links. We will not treat them as affiliate offers unless a program issues a tracked URL and we disclose it.
